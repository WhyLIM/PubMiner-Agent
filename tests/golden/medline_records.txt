AB  - BACKGROUND: The role of KRAS status in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) prognosis remains debated. METHODS: We analysed 412 patients undergoing curative resection across two centres. RESULTS: Multivariate analysis identified elevated CA 19-9 as an independent predictor of poor overall survival (HR 2.1, 95% CI 1.4-3.2). CONCLUSIONS: KRAS status alone was not associated with survival in this cohort.
